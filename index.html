What we collect

From you (the parent/guardian account holder): a display name you choose, and a record that you affirmed you are the parent or legal guardian of the children you add.

From your children's profiles, which you create and control: a nickname (not a full legal name), an age, a preset emoji avatar (never a photo), a manual "free to play" / "not available" status, and any busy-time calendar entries you add.

We do not collect your child's location, precise birthdate, full legal name, photo, or contact information. We do not collect location data from anyone, ever, under any circumstance.

Why we collect it

Every piece of data listed above exists to make the app's core function work: letting you signal your child's availability and busy times to families you've personally connected with. We don't use any of it for advertising, analytics profiling, or any purpose beyond running the app.

Who can see it

Your child's status, calendar, and play session activity are visible only to families you've explicitly connected with by sharing an invite link. There is no public directory, no search, and no way for a family to see your child's information without you first sharing it with them directly.

Removing a connection immediately and permanently cuts that family's visibility into your data, in both directions.

Where it's stored

All data is stored in Apple's iCloud/CloudKit infrastructure, tied to your own iCloud account. We don't run our own servers and don't have a separate company database — your data lives in your iCloud account and, once shared, in the private databases of the specific families you've connected with.

Third parties

We don't share any data with advertisers, analytics companies, or any other third party. There are no ad SDKs in this app.

Your rights

As the parent/guardian account holder, you can at any time:

  • Review everything stored about your account and children (Settings > Export My Data)
  • Delete your account and all associated data permanently (Settings > Delete Account & All Data)
  • Remove a friend connection to immediately cut that family's visibility into your data

If you have questions about your child's data or want help exercising these rights, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL — fill in before publishing].

Children's privacy (COPPA)

This app is designed for use by parents/legal guardians on behalf of their children. We collect the minimum information needed for the app to function, as described above, and require an affirmative statement from the account holder that they are the child's parent or legal guardian before any child profile can be created.
